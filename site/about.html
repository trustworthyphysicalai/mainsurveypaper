What we provide

A living survey of trustworthy Physical AI: the landscape, the life cycle, T-PAIO in practice, and the T-PAI framework. Physical AI is grounded in perception, reasoning, modeling, prediction, simulation, planning, and embodied action. It operates under real-time safety constraints. Checklists written for digital AI miss physical safety, cyber-physical security, and material circularity.

We update the paper on the first Monday of each month. A draft goes out the Sunday before. Open items live on the outstanding list. Anyone who contributes in Overleaf is named as an author. Read it on Our Work.

How we partner

Authors collaborate from universities and companies around the world. We take no corporate or institutional sponsorship. Donations cover operations and tools only — Slack, Google, and the like.

This work is for the healthy and safe development of technology. It is not meant to slow down good technology.
