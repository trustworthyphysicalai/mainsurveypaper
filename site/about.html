Trustworthy Physical AI

An independent research community.

Physical AI is grounded in perception, reasoning, modeling, prediction, simulation, planning, or embodied interaction. It operates under real-time safety constraints. Digital Trustworthy AI checklists miss physical safety, cyber-physical security, and material circularity.

We update the paper on the first Monday of each month. Anyone who contributes in Overleaf is named as an author. Write to trustworthyphysicalai@gmail.com.

Funding

We are a non-sponsored project. We do not take corporate or institutional sponsorship. We only accept donations to cover operations and technology subscriptions, such as Slack, Google, and similar tools. To help with operating costs, write to trustworthyphysicalai@gmail.com.
